Creamy Chicken Alfredo Pasta Recipe

Description

Chicken Alfredo Pasta is a creamy and delicious Italian-inspired dish made with tender chicken, fettuccine noodles, and a rich Parmesan cheese sauce. The combination of buttery pasta and flavorful chicken makes this meal perfect for lunch or dinner.

This easy homemade recipe uses simple ingredients and takes less than an hour to prepare. It is a great choice for family dinners or special occasions and tastes even better when served with garlic bread or a fresh salad.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz fettuccine pasta
  • 2 boneless chicken breasts
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • - Fresh parsley (optional)

Steps

  1. Prepare the Chicken: Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
  2. Cook the Chicken: Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the chicken until golden and fully cooked.
  3. Boil the Pasta: Cook the fettuccine noodles in salted boiling water until tender, then drain.
  4. Make the Sauce: Melt butter in a pan, add garlic and heavy cream, then stir in Parmesan cheese until smooth.
  5. Combine Everything: Add the cooked pasta and sliced chicken to the Alfredo sauce and mix well.
  6. 1. Serve and Enjoy: Garnish with parsley and extra Parmesan cheese before serving warm.
