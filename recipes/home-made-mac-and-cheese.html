Homemade Baked Mac and Cheese Recipe

Description

Baked Mac and Cheese is a warm and comforting dish made with tender macaroni pasta covered in a rich, creamy cheese sauce. The melted cheese and golden, crispy topping make every bite delicious and satisfying.

This homemade recipe is easy to make and requires only a few simple ingredients. It is perfect for family gatherings, holiday dinners, or a quick meal at home and can be served as a main dish or a tasty side.

Ingredients>

  • 2 cups elbow macaroni
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • - ½ cup breadcrumbs (optional)

Steps

  1. Cook the Pasta: Boil the macaroni in salted water until tender, then drain and set aside.
  2. Prepare the Butter Mixture: Melt butter in a saucepan and stir in flour until smooth.
  3. Make the Cheese Sauce: Slowly add milk and stir until thickened, then mix in the cheeses and seasonings.
  4. Combine the Ingredients: Add the cooked macaroni to the cheese sauce and stir until evenly coated.
  5. Bake the Mac and Cheese: Transfer to a baking dish, sprinkle breadcrumbs on top, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 20–25 minutes until bubbly and golden.
  6. 1. Serve and Enjoy: Let the mac and cheese cool for a few minutes before serving warm.
